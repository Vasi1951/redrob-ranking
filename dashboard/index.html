Senior AI Engineer Pune/Noida, IN Experience: 5-9 Years

Job Description: Senior AI Engineer — Founding Team

Company: Redrob AI (Series A talent intelligence platform)

We're looking for someone who is simultaneously comfortable with deep technical depth in modern ML systems (embeddings, retrieval, ranking, LLMs) and a scrappy product-engineering attitude.

Core Skills Required:

  • Production embeddings-based retrieval systems (sentence-transformers, OpenAI, BGE, E5)
  • Vector databases or hybrid search (Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, Milvus, FAISS, OpenSearch)
  • Strong Python and code quality
  • Evaluation frameworks for ranking systems (NDCG, MRR, MAP, A/B testing)

Anti-Patterns (Penalized):

  • Candidates who switch companies every 1.5 years (title-chasers)
  • LangChain framework-only enthusiasts without pre-LLM ML experience
  • Entire career spent solely at service consulting firms (TCS, Infosys, etc.)
  • Pure researchers without production deployment experience
